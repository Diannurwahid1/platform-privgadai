# Aset maskot untuk portal mobile

- Aset: `frontend/public/brand/privgadai-mascot-wave.png` (1024 × 1536 PNG RGBA, latar transparan).
- Referensi visual: gambar showcase yang disediakan pengguna pada 18 Oktober 2026.
- Status: **kandidat visual**. Wajah, warna, bentuk tubuh, dan gestur mengikuti contoh sedekat mungkin, tetapi generasi gambar tidak menghasilkan salinan piksel 100% dan perlu review brand sebelum dipakai sebagai aset final.
- Pemakaian yang direncanakan: onboarding, hero dashboard borrower, panduan foto aset, dan empty state; ukuran/crop tidak boleh menutupi tombol atau teks.

## Prompt generasi

> Using the attached PrivGadai mobile app showcase as the visual reference, create ONE isolated, full-body mascot character for reuse in the PrivGadai borrower and provider mobile interfaces. Match the mascot's recognizable design as closely as possible: charming compact glossy white 3D service robot, rounded oversized white helmet, glossy black dark-navy visor face, two bright electric-blue glowing crescent eyes and tiny smiling mouth, short rounded torso, small articulated arms and hands, short legs, subtle blue accent on top of helmet, polished soft blue reflections and premium product-render quality. Friendly welcoming pose with right hand raised in a wave, front three-quarter view. On chest leave a small clean blue geometric emblem area, avoid rendered letters or words. Keep entire robot visible including feet, centered, with generous transparent padding all around; no phone screen, no UI, no background, no text, no extra characters, no gradients outside the robot, no floor shadow. High resolution, crisp edges, alpha transparency. This is a character asset, not a screenshot recreation.

Logo resmi tetap menggunakan `frontend/public/privgadai-logo-dark.svg` dan `frontend/public/privgadai-logo-light.svg`. Evaluasi final maskot dilakukan bersama screenshot layar borrower/provider pada viewport acuan.
